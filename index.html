RX385 Activity Logger v6

Log audio activity while the RX385 scans channels automatically, or while monitoring one fixed channel. Microphone audio cannot reveal the current RF channel.

Safe setup: Use your computer microphone near the radio speaker. Don’t wire the radio’s speaker output into a computer input until the electrical levels and wiring have been checked.

Voice filtering and sound-duration gating may suppress nearby typing and running water, but they cannot identify whether sound came from the radio. Very short squelch pops may be missed with 120/250 ms gating.

Optional: run the faucet or type normally while sampling, with the RX385 silent. This measures a conservative noise threshold; it does not learn to recognize those sounds.

Scan mode: activity is logged with channel and privacy code marked Unknown. The microphone cannot read the radio display or identify RF channels.
